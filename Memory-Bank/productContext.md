# Product Context

## Problem Statement
Cryptocurrency traders need timely information about large market movements to make informed decisions. Whale wallets (large holders) and market makers significantly influence market conditions, but tracking their activities manually is time-consuming and often too slow to be actionable.

## Solution
A real-time monitoring system that:
- Tracks predefined whale/market maker wallets
- Analyzes transaction patterns and fund flows
- Generates trading signals based on significant movements
- Delivers alerts directly via Telegram

## User Experience Goals
- Simple setup process to receive alerts
- Clear, concise notifications with actionable context
- Customizable alert preferences
- Minimal noise (high signal-to-noise ratio)

## Target Audience
- Active cryptocurrency traders
- Trading groups and communities
- Market analysts
- DeFi participants

## Value Proposition
"Stay ahead of the market by receiving real-time alerts when whales and market makers make significant moves. Transform on-chain data into actionable trading signals delivered directly to your Telegram."
